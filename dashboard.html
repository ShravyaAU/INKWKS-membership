Student Print Pass

The Generous Devils Print Pass gives ASU students three supervised visits to the Basement Press Co. screen-printing studio for $25. Learn the process, use professional equipment, and bring a project from screen preparation to final print without buying an entire print shop yourself.

Each session is up to three hours. Your pass includes shared screen-printing tools, standard cleanup supplies, black ink, orientation, and one standard screen preparation and exposure. Bring your own shirts, paper, bags, or other approved printable items.

Specialty inks, transparencies, additional screens, artwork corrections, and replacement materials may require an additional fee. Studio access is subject to staffing, capacity, equipment condition, and completion of required safety training.

Before you purchase

Review the full studio-use, cancellation, expiration, cleanup, and safety policies. Passes are assigned to the purchaser and may not be shared. Completing payment does not guarantee a specific appointment time; sessions must be booked based on studio availability.

Important Notice

The pass does not guarantee a perfect final product. Screen printing involves testing, registration, setup, and the possibility of misprints.

Included in Pass
  • • Three supervised studio sessions (up to 3 hrs each)
  • • Shared tools, presses, and washout/drying equipment
  • • Standard black ink and basic cleanup supplies
  • • Reclaimed test paper when available
  • • Guidance from a trained studio monitor
  • Equipment Orientation: PENDING

One standard screen preparation and exposure is included with each three-session pass. Additional screens, artwork corrections, transparencies, and screen re-exposure may require an additional fee.

What to Bring
  • • Screen done ahead of time.
  • • Approved shirts, paper, bags, or other printable items
  • • Transparency paid at time of booking.
  • • An apron or clothes that can get dirty. We are not responsible.
  • • Any specialty ink or project-specific material not included in the pass
  • [!] Closed-toe shoes are required for studio entry.
