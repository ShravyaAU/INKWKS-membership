Basement Press Co. Studio Policy

Screen Reclaiming Policy

Members may not reclaim screens independently. Reclaiming is limited to authorized staff or students who have completed the required safety workshop, passed the skills check, and received studio approval. Screens remain the property of Generous Devils. Students must provide their own printable items.

Refunds & Access

Passes are nonrefundable. Participation is subject to studio capacity, equipment condition, staffing, and safety compliance.
